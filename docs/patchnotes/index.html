Path of Exile 2 Reveals New Shadow Class, Complete Campaign, and Conquerors of Wraeclast League Ahead of December 11 Full Release

November 7, Auckland, NEW ZEALAND - Path of Exile 2 reaches its monumental Full Release on December 11 (PST), bringing the long-awaited conclusion to its epic campaign with Acts 5 and 6. Master the deadly arts of stealth, daggers, and traps with the new Shadow class, and command the battlefield with the Knight, a regal new Ascendancy for the Duelist: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiJ_2QdjoWg

  • The Epic Conclusion and Full Release: Path of Exile 2's monumental Full Release brings together ten playable classes, 26 Ascendancy classes, over 320 skills, more than 400 Unique Items, and over 150 endgame maps.
  • Siege Rival Factions: With over 70 new points of interest and 23 campaign battles, players will have opportunities to rally powerful allies and defeat enemy factions in Path of Exile 2’s new league, Conquerors of Wraeclast.
  • Forge Your Build With Over 110 New and Reworked Uniques: Forge your path to unimaginable power with over 40 brand-new Unique Items, more than 70 revamped Uniques, a sweeping overhaul of the passive skill tree, major changes to skills and Ascendancies, and significant improvements to endgame progression and performance.

The Final Battle Awaits

In Act 5, players return to Oriath, a once-great city now ravaged by war, corruption, and the horrors that have taken hold within its walls. With the Twilight Order and the Beast threatening Wraeclast, players must carve a path through the city's fallen districts, confront the monstrosities lurking within, and breach its defences to pave the way for a full-scale assault. From the twisted slums and abandoned noble estates to the desecrated temple district and grand arena, the final acts will take players through some of the most ambitious environments and challenging boss encounters ever created for Path of Exile 2.

Strike from the Shadows with a Deadly New Class

From the darkest corners of Wraeclast emerges the Shadow, a deadly new class specialising in daggers, traps, and the art of stealth. A master of deception and assassination, the Shadow is able to stalk his prey unseen, strike with lethal precision, and vanish into darkness before his enemies can retaliate: https://youtu.be/FD-lo79LtAM

  • The Shadow brings a uniquely fast and fluid style of Stealth to the ARPG genre. Set and execute deadly ambushes in an Instant. Each successful kill resets the cooldown of Stealth, allowing players to chain deadly attacks, disappear into the shadows, and prepare their next ambush.
  • For those who prefer a more calculated approach, the Shadow can turn the battlefield into a deadly trap. While concealed, players can lay multiple Traps that detonate in the order they were placed upon leaving Stealth. With eight different trap types, players can unleash devastating combinations of impale, poison, fire, and ice, turning even the most formidable enemies into unsuspecting victims.
  • But the Shadow's mastery of darkness goes far beyond stealth and subterfuge. Summon Shades that are shadowy copies to deceive enemies and replicate their attacks.
    • Harness a new resource, Darkness, that empowers strikes while in stealth.
    • With Eclipse, players can temporarily enter the shadow realm, transforming every attack into a stealth strike and unleashing the combined might of their Shades upon their enemies.

The Shadow arrives with two powerful Ascendancy classes, the Assassin and Infiltrator, offering even more ways to master the darkness.

Lead the Charge with the Knight Ascendancy

Take up arms and lead your forces to glory with the Knight, a powerful new Ascendancy for the Duelist that commands the battlefield through martial prowess, War Banners, and mounted combat.

Never go into a battle alone, as this Ascendancy allows you to recruit a loyal Squire who can be outfitted with weapons and armor, that can use any melee Skill Gems. As a standard-bearer of Wraeclast's great empires, the Knight can wield powerful War Banners, each granting unique bonuses inspired by the different empires of Wraeclast. For instance, raise the Vaal standard to cover the battlefield in blood, empowering attacks and turning the tide of combat in your favour. And when the call to battle sounds, the Knight can unleash a thunderous Mounted Charge, riding a chariot headlong into enemy ranks, scattering foes, and stunning them in the process - making them unable to do any actions for a short time as they wander in drunken stupor.

With steel in hand and loyal companions at their side, the Knight stands ready to lead the charge and carve a path to victory. The Duelists’ second ascendancy, Master of Arms, will arrive at a later date.

Raise Your Armies, Siege the Castles, and Conquer Wraeclast

The drums of war echo across Wraeclast as Conquerors of Wraeclast, an all-new league, brings large-scale warfare, castle building, and territorial conquest to Path of Exile 2. Advancing your stronghold prowess will allow you to march upon enemy castles and deploy mighty siege engines to shatter fortress walls, send war elephants charging through enemy ranks, and unleash two-headed Chimeras to rain fire upon opposing forces: youtube.com/watch?v=NVLdP41VBfk&feature=youtu.be

Beneath the banners of war, a far darker threat is stirring. The rival Maraketh clan are bound by a sinister promise from Severa, an ancient vampire lord, whose followers seek to resurrect her through forbidden blood-infused rituals. To uncover the truth and put an end to her ambitions, players must conquer the three rival clans before marching upon Severa's own stronghold for a final confrontation.

Additional features include:

  • Reforging: A new crafting system that allows players to replace unwanted item modifiers with permanently fractured modifiers, including powerful new effects obtained through blood-infused materials.
  • Independent Commanders: Recruit up to three commanders to lead armies on Atlas missions, explore new territory, and collect rewards over time.
  • Faction Conquest: Join a faction and compete against rival players to capture and control 100 castles scattered across the Atlas. Each conquered territory grants increased item rarity, access to special Hunting Grounds, and strategic advantages that strengthen your faction's hold over Wraeclast.
New League: Conquerors of Wraeclast

Conquerors of Wraeclast introduces army recruitment, fortress construction, territorial conquest, and large-scale siege warfare:

  • Over 70 new points of interest.
  • 23 campaign battles.
  • Recruit soldiers, mercenaries, and powerful beasts.
  • Gather supplies and lead armies against rival factions.
  • Choose between three factions: Ezomytes, Maraketh, and Karui.
  • Construct and upgrade fortresses with over 20 buildings and upgrades.
  • Command armies of up to 1,000 soldiers.
  • Deploy siege engines, war elephants, and two-headed Chimeras.
  • Conquer rival strongholds and confront Severa, an ancient vampire lord.

Unleash New Powers, Unique Items, and Ascendancies

Over 200 new base item types have been added, along with 40 new Unique items and over 70 revamped Uniques that offer powerful modifiers, new abilities, and enhanced visuals.

The Passive Skill Tree has also undergone a massive overhaul, introducing hundreds of new stats and expanded support for a wide range of combat mechanics. Jewel Sockets have been relocated into passive clusters, while Support Gems have received extensive improvements, including rebalanced abilities.

For those seeking new ways to unleash destruction upon their enemies, new skills such as Molten Forge allow players to summon a blazing anvil and imbue their maces with molten power, unleashing fiery slams upon their foes. Meanwhile, Soul Extraction lets wielders of Chaos magic consume accumulated Decay to deliver devastating bursts of damage.

Several existing classes and Ascendancies have also received major reworks, including the Mercenary, Infernalist, Blood Mage, and Acolyte of Chayula. From commanding demonic companions and manipulating the blood of fallen enemies to summoning deadly clones, these changes introduce new abilities and exciting ways to play.

A Reforged Endgame and Improved Performance

The Atlas Passive Tree has been reforged into 70 more impactful nodes, giving players greater control over their endgame progression. Atlas Passive Points can now be earned progressively by completing objectives, defeating powerful bosses, and conquering special maps. Beyond the battlefield, significant improvements to the game's performance, delivering up to 25% additional CPU performance gains in the 1.0 update, alongside reduced memory usage and smoother frame pacing. The gates of Wraeclast are also opening to more adventurers. Path of Exile 2 is now Steam Deck Verified, allowing players to take their journey on the go. The game will also make its debut on Mac alongside the Full Release, supporting M2-series Macs and newer, M1 Pro systems.

Content Update 3.30.0 — Path of Exile: Covens of Umbra

Content Update 3.30.0 — Path of Exile: Covens of Umbra

The Covens of Umbra Challenge League

  • In the Covens of Umbra Challenge League, you will encounter wicked covenites performing gruesome rites to summon their Queen, seeking to plunge the world into unending darkness. Defeat them to divine the future of Wraeclast and try to bend its fate to your will.
  • Upon disrupting the coven's magicks, you will get to select a Fate for the boss of the area you're in. Each Fate has a handful of effects, increasing both the rewards and difficulty of the encounter.
  • A fully empowered boss can be quite a threatening fight, but the tantalising rewards of each Fate you invoke will surely make it worth it.
  • The twisted witchcraft of the Coven allows you to put the remains of your fallen enemies to use by placing them on your own Wickerman. Obtain Viscera from a defeated boss empowered by Fates to claim a scrap of their power for your own.
  • For more information about this expansion, check out pathofexile.com/covens
  • With 3.30.0, there are Standard, Hardcore and Solo Self-Found variations of the Covens of Umbra challenge league available. They have the same core mechanics and items. You can create private league versions of these leagues, with mods that make the game harder.
  • The Hardcore variations of Covens of Umbra are parented to their Standard Covens of Umbra equivalent, meaning if you die in Hardcore Covens of Umbra you can continue on in the Standard Covens of Umbra League.
  • The new Covens of Umbra challenge league includes a set of 40 new challenges, and 8 new challenges in Ruthless Covens of Umbra. There are microtransaction rewards for completing challenges that are only obtainable in this league. These rewards will be revealed in an upcoming news post.
  • From the 19th challenge onwards and for every third challenge after that, you will receive pieces of the Covens of Umbra Totem Pole decoration to display in your hideout. The Totem Pole permanently showcases how many of the Covens of Umbra challenges you completed during the league.

New Content and Features

  • Added a new Intelligence Skill, Arcane Link: Targets an allied player to apply a buff which links you to them for a duration. While linked, they deal additional lightning damage based on your mana. If the target dies while linked, you will also die. This skill cannot be triggered, or used by Totems, Traps, or Mines. It is offered to the Scion for completing Breaking the Seal, and can be purchased from Petarus and Vanja by the Marauder, Duelist and Templar.
  • Added a new Intelligence Transfigured Skill, Fireball of Radiation: Unleashes a ball of fire ahead that inflicts a short but powerful fire damage over time debuff to each enemy in an area around it.
  • Added 11 new Unique Items.
  • Added a new Jewel basetype, Mycelium Core.
  • Added 69 more foiled Tier 17 Valdo's Map options that the Valdo's Puzzle Box can yield.
  • Added 96 more foiled Unique Item options that the Voidborn Reliquary can yield.
  • Most Pinnacle content items, Fragments and Keys now have unique audio.
  • Continued to incrementally improve the sound, art, effects and environments.

Endgame Changes

  • The Shaped Region system has been completely reworked.
  • There are 10 types of Shaped Region, each associated with a specific type of Thread. By default your Atlas has a Shaped Region of each Thread type on it.
  • Shaped Regions grant affected Maps free Threads of that type. Successfully completing the Shaped Region objective in each Map increases the free Thread count by 1 in all other Maps affected by that Shaped Region.
  • Completing Shaped Region objectives no longer grants other Maps in that Shaped Region bonus modifiers.
  • While you have less than 10 Shaped Regions on your Atlas, you can click a button at the centre of the Atlas to Regenerate all Shaped Regions.
  • Shaped Regions applied by Astrolabes have a higher base number of free Threads applied to Maps in the Region.
  • Astrolabes have been changed to add a Shaped Region of the specified type to a specific Map on the Atlas, removing existing Shaped Regions of that type from the Atlas.
  • Astrolabes are no longer restricted from dropping in Shaped Regions.
  • Templar, Fruiting and Deceptive Astrolabes have been removed. Pre-existing items have been converted to a Cryptic Astrolabe which can be used or vendored to grant a random other Astrolabe.
  • Scarabs have been removed from the game. A new item type, Threads, have been added in their place.
  • Threads can be used when entering an Atlas area through the Map Device to apply the associated League mechanic to that Thread to the area. For each Thread expended, up to a maximum of eight, there are also increasingly higher and higher chances to apply powerful randomised transformative effects to the area related to that League mechanic.

  • The Atlas Passive Tree has been completely reworked. You now begin at the centre of the tree, and head outwards.
  • The total number of Atlas Passive Skill Points you can earn has been increased to 150 (previously 138).
  • The quest reward for defeating the Maven, Searing Exarch, Eater of Worlds and for each of Maven's six special Invitations is now 2 Atlas Skill points and 2 Atlas Respec points (previously 1 Atlas Skill point and 2 Atlas Respec points).
  • The quest reward for defeating the Uber Elder is now 4 Atlas Skill points and 2 Atlas Respec points (previously 1 Atlas Skill point and 2 Atlas Respec points).

  • Atlas Passive clusters related to Delve and Heist have been removed.
  • Atlas Passive clusters related to Secondary League mechanics have been reworked, and new clusters have been added. This includes Essence, Harvest, Mercenaries, Ore Deposits, Rogue Exiles, Shrines, Strongboxes, and Torment.

Item Generation Changes

  • Fundamentally changed how Items are generated in Path of Exile.
  • Previously, if a monster drops an item it rolls its "Rarity" (Normal, Magic, Rare or Unique) based on the monster's Item Rarity. If the "Rarity" was not Unique it would then pick from a list of Drop Pools such as Weapons, Rings, Scrolls of Wisdom, Skills Gems, Currency etc to select a specific item to drop.
  • For example, if the selected "Rarity" was Rare, and the selected Drop Pool was Weapons, then it would generate a random weapon and because the "Rarity" was Rare the weapon would generate as a Rare item. If the selected Drop Pool was Scrolls of Wisdom, then it would generate a Scroll of Wisdom. In this case the monster having more Item Rarity was mostly meaningless as if the selected "Rarity" was Normal, Magic or Rare the item generated would still be a Scroll of Wisdom.
  • Because of the above, Magic monsters (which have much higher Item Rarity compared to Normal monsters) had 50% less chance to drop items that can't have rarity, such as Currency or Skill Gems, while Rare and Uniques had 80% less chance. This was to prevent the experience of killing a difficult boss and having most of the items that dropped not care about the Item Rarity of that boss.
  • Item Rarity on monsters and chests has changed to now affect which Drop Pools can be selected. For example, the Scrolls of Wisdom and Portal Scrolls Drop Pools now can only be selected if the generated "Rarity" was normal. This means as monsters have more Item Rarity they will drop fewer Scrolls of Wisdom and Portal Scrolls.
  • Currency item Drop Pools have been split into a Magic Currency (such as Orbs of Transmutation, Orbs of Alteration, Jeweller's Orbs) and Rare Currency (such as Orbs of Alchemy, Chaos Orbs, Divine Orbs and Mirrors of Kalandra). Magic Currency can only generate if the selected "Rarity" was Magic, and Rare Currency can only generate if the selected "Rarity" was Rare. What this amounts to is the more Item Rarity a monster has, the better the Currency items it drops.
  • Because high Item Rarity on Monsters now excludes common currencies and increases the chance for valuable currencies, we have removed 50% less chance to drop items that can't have rarity from Magic Monsters, and 80% less from Rare and Unique monsters. As a result, a much greater percentage of valuable currency items you find will now be coming from Rare and Unique Monsters.
  • Rare and Magic Monster modifiers no longer add Quantity of Items Dropped to Monsters.
  • The amount of Rarity of Items Dropped granted by Rare and Magic Monster modifiers has been increased.
  • Rare Monsters now have slightly more inherent Item Quantity.
  • Monsters with high Item Rarity will now have a higher chance to drop rarer Divination Cards. Certain common Divination Cards will be restricted from dropping from monsters with very high Item Rarity.
  • Removed the Expedition Remnant for increased Item Quantity from Monsters.
  • For the following changes where Item Quantity has been removed, Item Rarity has been increased to compensate:

  • Reworked how Modifier Tier Rating works to address two longstanding issues. For context, Modifier Tier Rating is a system used throughout the game to generate Magic and Rare items with higher tier Modifiers.
