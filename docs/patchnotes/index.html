1.0.0 Patch Notes

poe(2)db · source ↗

Version 1.0.0

  • Release on December 11th PDT
  • Free and without requiring a minimum of $30.
  • Balance of the game in a good place
  • Endgame in a place that people are enjoying
  • New Core Supporter Pack
  • New Acts: Act5 and Act 6
  • New weapons: One Hand Swords and Two Hand Swords
  • New character class: Duelist
  • New Challenge league
Wishing Well
  • Seamless Keyboard/Controller Switching
  • Two-Factor Authentication
  • Death recap
  • All 12 classes (Shadow, Marauder, Templar) and all 3 ascendancy classes (Druid, Ranger).
  • Divination cards
  • Trial of the Ancestors (3rd ascendancy option)

ExileCon 2026

Exilecon 2026 will be hold on November 7-8 (NZT) and full information on version 1.0.0 will be available.
